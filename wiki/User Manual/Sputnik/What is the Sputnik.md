## Introduction for the Sputnik

The Sputnik is by far the most complicated part of the Cosmonautics mod, but luckily it's concept is pretty easy to understand and you won't require highly complicated code for most of your rockets, so don't be afraid to learn everything about it. And you also won't *necessarily* need the sputnik nodes to control your spacecraft thanks to CC:Tweaked computers, but it's still recommended to understand the sputnik.

In the chapters of the sputnik, you can find the nodes, controls and various functions built-in in the sputnik. All also accessible via the Manual Lobby.

Please checkout as first lesson "[GUI Controls](/wiki/User%20Manual/Sputnik/GUI%20Controls.md)".
