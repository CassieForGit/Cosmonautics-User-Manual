## The Measurements of Objects

There are many measurements in the world, some simple and important, some strange and scary. We'll focus on the important things.

### Length  

Length is a measurement of a distance between two points. it can be one, two, or tri dimensional.
In minecraft the main way of measuring things is via blocks, and one block equals to one meter. All normal lengths in the manual are going to be in meters.
A length between two points on the same plane is a two dimensional line. It's direction is expressed in an angle relative to the horizontal line (or any line).
A length between two points on two different planes is a 3 dimensional line. It's direction is expressed in two angles instead of one.

### Area

An Area is a space defined by a perimeter and is measured in meters square ( M^2 ) ( M\*M = M^2 ).
It's two lengths multiplied by each other. (L1\*L2 = A)

### Volume

A volume is a 3 dimensional measurement defined by surface areas and is measured in meters cube ( M^3 ) ( M\*M\*M = M^2).
It's 3 lengths multiplied by each other. (L1\*L2\*L3 = V)
It can also be measured in Liters.

### Force

A Force is expressed via a vector. A vector is a 3 dimensional line which also holds an amount (called magnitude).
An "amount of force" is the magnitude of the vector.
An example of Force is Weight.
Forces are measured in Newtons.

### Angles

An angle can be measured in Radians or in Degrees.
