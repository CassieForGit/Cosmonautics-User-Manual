## The Basic Principles of Physics

Some Principles are the 4 Fundamental forces of flight, those being Weight, Thrust, Drag and Lift.
Weight is a Force acted via an object's mass. 
Thrust is a Force acted via various ways, one being chemical reactions and pressure.
Drag is an Aerodynamic Force acted upon a body via a gas and isn't opposite to the Weight of the body
Lift is an Aerodynamic Force acted upon a body via a gas and is opposite to the Weight of the body

There is also magnetism, an electric force.
